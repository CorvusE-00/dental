These Open Graph and Twitter Card images were captured on 29 September 2026 from the local production build at a
1200 × 630 output size. The capture used the rendered desktop homepage at a wider CSS viewport with a 0.75 device
scale factor so the hero image includes both people while preserving the page's actual typography, navigation, copy,
and visual system. Browser chrome, scrollbars, and mobile browser controls are not included.
